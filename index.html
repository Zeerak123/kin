Kin Privacy Policy

Draft

This Privacy Policy explains how Kin: Family Care Coordinator ("Kin", "we", "us") collects, uses and discloses personal information through its mobile application and related services (the "Service"). By agreeing to this Privacy Policy, you consent to these practices for information already held in the Service and information added later.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information provided by you and other members of your care circle, including account details (name, email address and credentials), information about the person receiving care, and care information such as medications, dose records, appointments, notes, health observations, messages and visitor check-ins. We also collect technical information needed to operate the Service, such as time zone, device and notification identifiers, IP addresses, and sign-in and usage records.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal information, including care information, to:

  • provide and maintain the Service, including reminders and notifications;
  • respond to inquiries and provide support;
  • analyze how the Service is used, and evaluate, develop and improve its features and reliability;
  • protect the Service and the people who use it, including by detecting and investigating misuse, fraud, security incidents and risks to safety; and
  • comply with the law and enforce our agreements.

Access to identifiable care information is limited to authorized personnel who reasonably require it for these purposes. Where practical, we use aggregated or de-identified information and do not attempt to re-identify it. You may ask us not to use your identifiable care information to improve the Service, without affecting your use of Kin.

We do not sell personal information or use care information for advertising.

3. How We Disclose Information

We disclose personal information:

  • to members of your care circle and, to the extent needed to join it or submit a visitor check-in, to anyone who has its join code;
  • to service providers that host data and deliver notifications on our behalf, which may process it in the United States and other countries, where it is subject to local law. Notifications may include names and medication names;
  • where required or permitted by law, or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of any person;
  • in connection with a transfer of the Service, subject to this Privacy Policy; and
  • with your consent.

4. Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as necessary for the purposes described above, or as required by law. You may delete your account in the app at any time. Information you contributed to a care circle remains available to its members after you leave, unless you request its removal. Residual copies may remain in backups for a limited period.

5. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or request a copy or the deletion of your personal information, and withdraw your consent, through the app or by contacting us. Withdrawing consent may prevent us from providing the Service. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, including the right to complain to a privacy authority. We will respond as required by applicable law.

6. Consumer Health Data (United States)

In Washington, Nevada, Connecticut and certain other states, care information may be consumer health data. We collect the categories described in Section 1 from you, other members of your care circle and visitors, use them for the purposes in Section 2, and share them only as described in Section 3. We have no affiliates and do not sell consumer health data. You may contact us to access or delete your consumer health data, learn with whom it has been shared, withdraw your consent, or appeal a decision we make about your request.

7. Security

We maintain reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit and at rest. No system is completely secure. We will provide notice of a breach where required by law.

8. Important Information

Kin is a coordination tool. It does not provide medical advice, supervise care, or act as a monitoring or emergency service, and our ability to review information does not create an obligation to do so. Reminders may be delayed or fail and should not be relied upon as the only means of ensuring that care is given. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Before entering information about another person, you must have that person's consent or the legal authority to act for them. The Service is intended for adults.

9. Changes and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of material changes in the app and, where required, request your consent.
